ACCOUNT RECOVERY

Forgot your login?

For your security, login details can only be reset on the computer that has the Hodman website files.

  1. Open the hodman website folder on your computer.
  2. Double-click Reset Hodman Admin Password.bat.
  3. Follow the prompts to choose the admin account and create a new username and password.
  4. Return here and sign in with the new details.

The password you type in the reset window is hidden. Choose at least 8 characters.
